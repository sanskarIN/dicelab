# DiceLab Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DiceLab aims to be a welcoming open-source project where people can collaborate without harassment or intimidation. Participate with patience, technical curiosity, and respect for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and code rather than attacking people;
- giving specific, constructive review feedback;
- respecting accessibility, privacy, and security concerns;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- avoiding spam, harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or disclosure of private information;
- respecting maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and project quality.

## Where this applies

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces officially used for DiceLab collaboration.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Include enough context for a fair review, but do not publicly repost sensitive personal information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on restoring a safe, productive environment.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment and personal attacks are not.

## Privacy

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Information may be shared only with people needed to review and resolve the situation or when required by law.
